# AI Text Detector

Detect whether text is **human-written**, **AI-generated**, or **humanized AI text**.

## Model Details

- **Architecture**: DistilBERT (distilbert-base-uncased)
- **Training Data**: 174,615 samples
  - 148,051 human-written texts (Wikipedia, The Pile, Reddit)
  - 6,160 AI-generated texts (Venice AI)
  - 20,404 humanized AI texts
- **Test Accuracy**: 99.81%
- **F1 Score**: 99.81%

## Classes

1. **Human-Written** - Text written by humans
2. **AI-Generated** - Text generated by AI models
3. **Humanized AI** - AI-generated text that has been modified to appear more human-like

## Usage

Simply paste your text into the input box and click "Analyze" to detect the origin of the text.

## Training

The model was trained on Google Colab with a T4 GPU for approximately 2 hours and 12 minutes.
